# Hackster Niko and the FBI Bunny Moon Funland

The next morning, Niko drove his buggy over a lunar ridge. And stopped.

Below him, spread across a huge Moon crater, was the strangest amusement park
he had ever seen. Roller coasters twisted through glass tunnels. A giant Ferris
wheel slowly turned beneath the stars. Moon buggies raced around a track. A huge
glowing carrot stood in the center. And across the entrance:

**FBI BUNNY MOON FUNLAND** / **EVERY RIDE IS DEFINITELY SAFE**

Niko stared. His little Stuffed Flopsy sat in the passenger seat wearing the
hacker hoodie Niko had made for him.

"That is exactly what somebody says when every ride is a trap." Stuffed Flopsy
said nothing. Niko nodded. "Agreed."

Radio: "I CAN SEE YOU." — "Hi, Flopsy." — "TURN AROUND." — "No." — "We are
CLOSED." The gate sign changed: **WELCOME, NIKO!** "Your sign disagrees."
Agent Cotton: "He got you there." — "COTTON!" Niko hit the accelerator.
"Let's ride."

## Ride One: The Carrot Comet
Nobody stopped him at the entrance, which worried him. Agent Flopsy ran the
coaster in an operator hat ("I am supervising"), Cotton supervised him from the
booth with popcorn. The train climbed to the glass dome; Earth appeared. Then it
stopped at the top, rotated upside down, and ran backward through loops. Niko
laughed instead of panicking. At a three-way split the car offered LEFT / RIGHT /
CARROT. "Carrot is not a direction." He pressed CARROT. It was the maintenance
shortcut and dumped him at the exit. Cotton: "You named the ride."

## Ride Two: The Moon Bumper Buggies
A dozen buggies turned toward him at once. Low gravity turned every bump into a
launch. Flopsy: "Stop using the bumps to jump!" — "Then don't put this on the
Moon!" Surrounded, Niko found two buttons: HORN and EMERGENCY RETURN. The horn
triggered PEDESTRIAN WARNING DETECTED and every robot buggy retreated. He drove
through them holding the horn down. Cotton had programmed it. "Safety."

## Ride Three: The Ferris Wheel Surprise
Clear bubble cabins, a slow peaceful turn, Earth on the horizon — no trap, which
made him suspicious. At the top the bubble detached, became a hamster ball, and
rolled down a rail onto the Moon surface, bouncing into a crater and launching
off the far side. Niko floated, delighted. He found steering paddles, took a
maintenance ramp, and bounced past the control booth waving. "Eleven out of ten."

## Ride Four: Moon Monster Manor
"THERE ARE ABSOLUTELY NO FAKE ALIENS INSIDE." Every monster was a Bunny Bot in a
costume; Niko called each one instantly. Then a real twenty-foot six-legged
mechanical monster charged and chased him through the house. Cornered at a locked
control room, he spotted a sign on its leg: PARK VEHICLE — MUST STOP FOR GUESTS.
He stepped into its path. GUEST IN PATH / EMERGENCY STOP. It halted six inches
away, and he walked backward to the exit while it helplessly followed.

## Ride Five: The Carrot Drop
A very tall, very thin, very suspicious tower. The seats shot up, the Moon base
spread below, and the drop was gentle in low gravity — until hidden thrusters
fired and slammed them down, up, down, sideways. Niko screamed, laughed, screamed.
At the bottom, hair straight up: "Terrible." Flopsy's ears drooped. "Do it again."

## Trap Six: The Moon Funhouse
Tilting floors, warping mirrors, then the mirrors began to move and formed a maze.
Slippery floor, swinging padded hammers, a spinning tunnel, and a pie cannon on a
moving walkway. A pie splattered a mirror — so Niko threw pies at every mirror
until only the real openings were uncovered, and mapped the maze with whipped
cream. Cotton: "Delicious cartography."

## The Final Ride: Flopsy's Impossible Roller Coaster
An unmarked black train. "The one you can't beat." Three months of work. Niko
buckled Stuffed Flopsy in beside him. The ride combined every old trap: an
underground loop, a wall of lasers, a splash pool, a wind tunnel, foam meteors, a
grabbing robot alien, a zero-gravity section — then the car detached into a Moon
buggy through a moving maze and re-locked onto rails.

In a dark room the track stopped. One light. A table. On it: a spoon. "Go ahead."
He reached — and four transparent cages dropped. CLANG. CLANG. CLANG. CLANG.
"You went for the spoon!" — "I knew it was a trap." — "But you still went for it!"
— "It's the spoon."

No sensors, no controls, no ducks, no potato. Flopsy had remembered everything.
Niko squeezed Stuffed Flopsy — SQUEAK — then pulled off its tiny hoodie. Beneath
was the hotel souvenir tag, and taped to it, a hotel keycard. He slid it into a
crack by the seat. BEEP. The park reused the hotel's ride-control system to save
money. RIDE EMERGENCY RESET. Every cage opened. "Mini Flopsy saves the day." —
"I SAVED YOU?!" — "Technically." He grabbed the spoon and ran.

## Escape
Past the haunted house, through the Funhouse, around the buggies, Flopsy bouncing
after him, Cotton in a tiny park cart. At the gate Flopsy demanded the score.
"Twenty out of ten. Best one yet." The final coaster: excellent. The spoon: dirty
trick. "And Mini Flopsy gets five bonus points." — "I BUILT THE PARK!"

Niko drove off with the toy in the passenger seat. The sign flickered:
**TODAY'S HIGH SCORE — NIKO 20 / FLOPSY 0.** Cotton stepped quietly backward.
"NIIIIIIIIIKOOOOOOOO!"

Far away, Niko squeezed the toy. SQUEAK. "Same time tomorrow?" His laptop beeped:
**MOON FUNLAND EXPANSION UNDER CONSTRUCTION — NEW ATTRACTION: THE IMPOSSIBLE
WATER PARK.** He looked at the cranes and smiled. "Oh, Flopsy. Now you're asking
for trouble."

**THE END… FOR NOW.**
